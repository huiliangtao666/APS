KBD01 — CONFIRMED IN ALL8 SCENARIOS
Expected contract: keyboard problem activation leaves visible useful focus at
its control, or moves focus to an appropriate visible task/detail target.
Actual: correct A-MIX/A-FILL tasks and exact overlap[20,30) are selected, but
activeElement remains the original problem BUTTON with :focus-visible=true,
while the whole button is outside the900px viewport. At640 its top/bottom are
-854.172/-775.172px; at1100 -618.531/-539.531px. Same values with/no banner and
normal/delayed requests. It is visible and focused before Enter. Screenshot
640-no-banner-0ms-problem.png shows the scrolled timeline while the focus
rectangle belongs to the absent problem button; DOM/rectangles prove position.
Source support: TimelinePanel.tsx selected-task scrollIntoView effect at33
explains the scroll without corresponding focus transfer. No patch attempted.
This is a focus presentation failure; selection/geometry remain correct.

KBD02 — CONFIRMED IN ALL8 SCENARIOS, INCLUDING NO-BANNER CONTROLS
Expected contract: after the asynchronous check completes, retain/restore
useful visible keyboard focus, or deliberately focus an appropriate result.
Temporary busy disabling is legitimate; persistent BODY focus is the failure.
Before Enter: target BUTTON active and :focus-visible=true. Mutation trace
shows BODY as soon as that button becomes disabled. Delayed cases confirm
BODY during the real verify request; focus is not restored after verify and
plans refresh. Completion and150ms settled samples both show BODY, target
inactive/:focus-visible=false, and disabled=false. No intervening user key,
mouse or artificial focus action accounts for the change. Normal fast cases
can finish before the immediate sample; the mutation trace still records the
busy disabled state. This is why a single after-Enter screenshot is insufficient.
The completion screenshot1100-no-banner-350ms-check-completed.png shows the
re-enabled check control in view without its prior focus indicator.
App.tsx perform sets busy before work and clears it in finally; verifyPlan awaits
verify and refresh. TaskLinkage disables the check control while busy; no
restoration is present. BODY during disabling is browser behavior, not by
itself a new browser defect. The confirmed product gap is lack of a useful
post-completion focus destination under the dispatched keyboard contract.

KBD03 — CONFIRMED IN ALL8 SCENARIOS USING NATIVE ACCESSIBILITY TREE
Expected contract: saved-plan combobox has an associated meaningful Chinese
name identifying the control; current option value is separate from its name.
Actual DOM: labels.length0, no aria-label, aria-labelledby or title; no wrapping

name.value="", while value.value="当前计算" before choosing a saved plan.

pickerDOM and pickerARIA is retained per scenario in independent/report.json.
App.tsx139 renders the unnamed select. Do not treat its concatenated option
innerText as a name. No broad whole-app accessibility audit is claimed.
