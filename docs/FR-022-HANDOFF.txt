Joint candidate search, capacity and rollback
The existing constructive scheduler now checks machine calendar/downtime/nonoverlap, all active explicit resource claims, personnel and material together at each candidate. A shift from tooling/space/personnel/supply causes the loop to restart and recheck all conditions; fixed operation duration remains unchanged. Tooling/space shortages retry at configured reservation-release events; personnel retries use group-availability and reservation-release events adjusted by participation offset. These are candidate searches, not optimization or proof of earliest/global feasibility.
Tooling/space use exact rational quantities from FR020 for every event-sweep capacity comparison. All same-resource claims of the candidate, including distinct FR021 members, count simultaneously. One member cannot acquire an entire batch's shared tooling for everyone. Two sets allow two parallel tasks, never three.600kg+600kg exceeding1000kg pushes the second explicit occupied interval past release. Numeric display rounding is never used for admission.
Personnel allocation now measures peak simultaneous use at half-open interval events rather than summing all reservations that touch different parts of a participation window. It still honors configured group, skill, shift, count and group availability; a machine's longer automatic cycle does not extend personnel availability. Greedy group selection can leave alternatives unexplored.
Only after ALL checks and the full-operation horizon succeed are material, personnel, tooling, space and machine reservations committed. Material previews and candidate resource arrays have no persistent effect. Resource shortage, material shortage or horizon/calendar failure leaves no phantom reservation for that failed operation or resource batch. Previously successful upstream operations retain their legitimate allocations; whole-order rollback is not claimed.
FR021's equal splits, original-order production/resource identities, exact material conservation and fixed-duration cycles remain intact. A shared resource cycle receives every member's declarations separately. Saved tooling/space rows link to the specific original production member; shared machine/personnel cycle rows link to the cycle's member tasks. Stock and source declarations are unchanged by scheduling; reservations are plan-local simulations, not cross-plan inventory/WIP or real return events.
